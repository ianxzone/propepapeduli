## 🌐 BAGIAN A: VERSI BAHASA INGGRIS (Target: Scopus Q1/Q2)

### 1. Introduction

#### 1.1 Background and Digital Ecosystem Transformation
The post-pandemic era has radically reshaped the educational landscape, accelerating the transition from voluntary, piecemeal software adoptions toward systematic, municipal-scale digital transformations (Bhattacherjee & Lin, 2015; Venkatesh et al., 2012). In emerging economies such as Indonesia, this transition intersects with comprehensive national curriculum overhauls—most notably the *Kurikulum Merdeka* and its cornerstone, the Pancasila Student Profile Strengthening Project (*Projek Penguatan Profil Pelajar Pancasila* or P5). While conventional Learning Management Systems (LMS) primarily prioritize asynchronous content repositories and multiple-choice summative assessments, modern character- and project-based educational frameworks demand dynamic, multi-stage pedagogical platforms capable of fostering critical reflection, interactive argument mapping, and real-time collaborative inquiry (Dwivedi et al., 2019; Tam & Oliveira, 2016).

In response to this demand, local educational authorities and academic researchers initiated **ProPePa PEDULI**, an integrated EdTech platform ecosystem structured around a six-phase pedagogical cycle: *Pelajari* (Learn), *Eksplorasi* (Explore), *Diskusi* (Discuss), *Ungkapkan* (Express), *Lakukan* (Act), and *Introspeksi* (Introspect). Uniquely deployed across all primary schools (*Sekolah Dasar*) within Cimahi City, West Java, the platform integrates micro-gamification mechanics (points, badges, leaderboards) with SVG-driven interactive argument boards and automated teacher feedback workflows. However, deploying a government-endorsed digital platform at the municipal scale introduces complex organizational and behavioural dynamics that extend far beyond initial software deployment.

#### 1.2 The Practical Problem: The Post-Adoption Churn in Public EdTech
From a digital business and public service ecosystem perspective, initial technology adoption (*first-time use*) driven by administrative mandates often masks severe underlying risks of user disengagement. Educational literature consistently highlights the phenomenon of "post-adoption disillusionment" or churn, wherein educators initially mandated to adopt new educational platforms gradually revert to legacy analogue routines or disjointed instant-messaging tools once the initial supervisory pressure wanes (Bhattacherjee, 2001; Mailizar et al., 2021). Primary school teachers face acute administrative cognitive loads; if an EdTech platform imposes high effort expectancy without tangible performance gains or intrinsic gratification, long-term continuance intention declines precipitously. Therefore, understanding the determinants that drive sustained, voluntary continuance intention among educators is paramount for the strategic sustainability and economic return on public educational investments.

#### 1.3 Theoretical Gaps in the Literature
Although technology adoption in education has been extensively explored, existing scholarship suffers from three critical limitations:
1. **The Trap of Initial Adoption vs. Sustained Continuance:** Most prior studies rely on the standard Technology Acceptance Model (TAM) or basic UTAUT to examine pre-adoption expectations or immediate uptake. Yet, in platform business models, long-term viability hinges on post-adoption continuance (*Continuance Intention*), an area best illuminated by synthesizing expectation confirmation dynamics with behavioral drivers (Bhattacherjee, 2001; Venkatesh et al., 2012).
2. **Scarcity of Municipal-Scale Empirical Evidence:** The majority of EdTech empirical evaluations are confined to isolated pilot experiments, single-school case studies, or university-level cohorts. Rigorous empirical evaluations of city-wide primary education ecosystems—encompassing diverse socioeconomic demographics and varying school infrastructural readiness—remain remarkably scarce.
3. **Under-theorized Role of Gamified Micro-Interactions in Teacher Continuance:** While gamification has been widely studied from the student's learning perspective, its influence as a hedonic and utilitarian stimulus affecting teacher satisfaction, perceived value, and habitual routine in public EdTech platforms remains poorly addressed in digital business literature.

#### 1.4 Research Objectives and Contributions
To bridge these theoretical and empirical chasms, this study investigates the structural mechanisms influencing primary school teachers' continuance intention toward the ProPePa PEDULI platform. Drawing upon the theoretical synthesis of the **Extended Unified Theory of Acceptance and Use of Technology (UTAUT2)** (Venkatesh et al., 2012) and Bhattacherjee's (2001) **Expectation-Confirmation Model (ECM)**, this study pursues the following core research questions:
- **RQ1:** How does expectation confirmation influence teachers' perceived performance expectancy and overall user satisfaction in a municipal-scale EdTech implementation?
- **RQ2:** To what extent do utilitarian factors (performance expectancy, effort expectancy), hedonic factors (gamification and visual interactivity), and facilitating conditions predict sustained continuance intention?
- **RQ3:** Does user habit act as a decisive behavioral anchor cementing platform retention among public school educators?

**Key Contributions:**
- *Theoretical Contribution:* We present a validated structural model contextualizing the post-adoption behavior of educators within municipal-scale public-private EdTech partnerships, bridging information systems continuance theory with public digital service management.
- *Practical & Managerial Contribution:* For educational policymakers and digital platform developers, our findings delineate the specific system design features and organizational support mechanisms required to avert post-mandate platform abandonment, ensuring financial and operational sustainability for smart city education initiatives.

## 🇮🇩 BAGIAN B: VERSI BAHASA INDONESIA (Target: SINTA 2)

### 1. Pendahuluan

#### 1.1 Latar Belakang dan Transformasi Ekosistem Bisnis Digital Pendidikan
Era pasca-pandemi telah mendorong akselerasi transformasi digital yang luar biasa pada sektor pendidikan formal di Indonesia. Pergeseran ini tidak lagi bersifat insidental, melainkan terstruktur melalui kebijakan nasional Kurikulum Merdeka yang menekankan pembelajaran kontekstual berbasis proyek melalui Projek Penguatan Profil Pelajar Pancasila (P5) (Mailizar et al., 2021). Perubahan paradigma pedagogi ini menuntut ketersediaan platform digital yang tidak sekadar berfungsi sebagai repositori dokumen pasif, melainkan mampu memfasilitasi siklus refleksi karakter, diskusi interaktif, serta evaluasi kemandirian siswa secara terukur dan berkelanjutan (Dwivedi et al., 2019; Tam & Oliveira, 2016).

Sebagai jawaban atas tantangan tersebut, platform **ProPePa PEDULI** dikembangkan sebagai solusi *EdTech Software-as-a-Service (SaaS)* yang mengintegrasikan alur pedagogi enam tahap: Pelajari, Eksplorasi, Diskusi, Ungkapkan, Lakukan, dan Introspeksi (PEDULI). Keunikan implementasi platform ini terletak pada jangkauannya yang diterapkan secara menyeluruh di seluruh Sekolah Dasar (SD), baik negeri maupun swasta, di Kota Cimahi, Jawa Barat. Platform ini memadukan mekanika gamifikasi (poin, lencana, papan peringkat), pemetaan argumen berbasis grafis vektor (SVG), serta dasbor monitoring guru untuk pelaporan nilai proyek.

#### 1.2 Masalah Praktis: Fenomena *Post-Adoption Churn* pada Layanan Digital Publik
Dalam perspektif bisnis digital dan tata kelola layanan publik (*public digital service*), adopsi teknologi yang didorong oleh instruksi dinas/pemerintah sering kali mengalami fenomena *post-adoption churn* atau pengabaian sistem setelah fase sosialisasi berakhir (Bhattacherjee, 2001). Guru-guru di tingkat sekolah dasar memiliki beban kerja administratif yang tinggi. Apabila platform digital yang disediakan dirasa rumit (*effort expectancy* tinggi), tidak memberikan efisiensi nyata pada penilaian (*low performance expectancy*), atau minim memberikan kepuasan kerja, para guru cenderung akan kembali ke metode pencatatan manual atau beralih ke aplikasi pesan instan konvensional. Oleh sebab itu, mempertahankan niat penggunaan berkelanjutan (*continuance intention*) menjadi faktor penentu utama keberhasilan investasi transformasi digital skala kota.

#### 1.3 Kesenjangan Penelitian (*Research Gap*)
Kajian ilmiah mengenai adopsi teknologi pendidikan di Indonesia sejauh ini masih menghadapi sejumlah kesenjangan:
1. **Keterbatasan Fokus pada *Initial Adoption*:** Mayoritas riset sistem informasi di Indonesia masih berkutat pada penerimaan awal menggunakan model TAM sederhana. Sangat sedikit studi yang meneliti faktor pendorong keberlanjutan pemakaian (*continuance intention*) setelah sistem digunakan dalam ritme kerja nyata sekolah.
2. **Keterbatasan Skala Empiris (*Sample Scope*):** Sebagian besar penelitian terdahulu hanya mengambil lokus pada satu sekolah percontohan atau beberapa kelas uji coba kecil dengan jumlah sampel di bawah 50 responden. Studi empiris komprehensif yang memotret adopsi platform digital serentak pada tingkat satu kota otonom (seluruh SD di Kota Cimahi) masih sangat langka di literatur manajemen sistem informasi nasional.
3. **Integrasi Teori Adopsi dan Konfirmasi Ekspektasi:** Masih terdapat keterbatasan sintesis antara teori adopsi perilaku lanjutan (**UTAUT-2**) dan teori konfirmasi kepuasan (**ECM**) dalam menjelaskan fenomena retensi pengguna pada ekosistem platform edukasi berbasis karakter.

#### 1.4 Tujuan Penelitian dan Kontribusi
Penelitian ini bertujuan untuk menganalisis faktor-faktor penentu yang memengaruhi niat penggunaan berkelanjutan (*continuance intention*) platform ProPePa PEDULI pada kalangan guru Sekolah Dasar di Kota Cimahi. Dengan mengintegrasikan kerangka **UTAUT-2** dan **Expectation-Confirmation Model (ECM)**, penelitian ini menguji hubungan antara konfirmasi ekspektasi, ekspektasi kinerja, kemudahan sistem, motivasi hedonik/gamifikasi, kepuasan pengguna, dan kebiasaan terhadap retensi platform.

Hasil riset ini diharapkan memberikan kontribusi teoretis dalam memperkaya literatur perilaku konsumen digital (*digital consumer behavior*) pada ekosistem layanan publik, serta memberikan kontribusi praktis bagi dinas pendidikan dan pengembang platform *EdTech* dalam merumuskan strategi retensi pengguna dan tata kelola platform digital yang berkelanjutan di era *smart governance*.
